# Cookies Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-22

This policy explains Bub's approach to cookies and similar local storage technologies.

## Mobile App Storage

The Bub mobile app may use secure local storage and platform caches to keep sign-in tokens, settings preferences, recent chat cache entries, and media cache entries available on your device.

## Web Or Admin Surfaces

If Bub provides web or admin surfaces, cookies or similar browser storage may be used for sign-in, security, preferences, analytics, or reliability.

## Managing Storage

You can clear app storage through your device settings or sign out where supported. Clearing storage may remove local preferences and cached content, but server-side account or tether data may remain until deleted through app features.

## Updates

Bub may update this policy as storage behavior changes. The app exposes the current policy version for reference.
