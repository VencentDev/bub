# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-22

This policy summarizes how Bub handles information used to run the app.

## Information We Process

Bub may process account profile details from sign-in, tether status, messages, uploaded media, Safe media metadata, moments, reactions, notifications, settings preferences, and basic operational logs.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate you, connect tethered partners, deliver chat and Safe features, sync settings, show notifications, maintain reliability, and protect the service from abuse.

## Shared Spaces

Content in chat, Safe, and moments is shared with your active tethered partner according to the feature being used. Removing a tether can permanently delete shared history for that tether.

## Retention

Bub keeps information while it is needed to provide the app and deletes tether-scoped records when destructive untethering or deletion features are completed.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Bub team through the support channel provided in the app or project site.
